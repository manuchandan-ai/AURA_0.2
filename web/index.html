Our Mission & Architecture

About AURA 2.0

Engineered to replace robotic, impersonal bots with an articulate, empathetic, and proactive cognitive partner that helps you achieve goals, maintain momentum, and master complex domains.

The Autonomous Companion Vision

Human-grade synergy between voice, roadmaps, and local memory

Most AI assistants today are reactive text prompts that forget your goals the moment you close the tab. AURA was created with a fundamentally different premise: intelligence should feel human, preserve your privacy, and actively keep you moving forward.

By uniting real-time turn-taking speech synthesis, automated goal decomposition, streak protection shields, and local vector RAG, AURA delivers an intimate, high-velocity workbench for creators, engineers, and operators.

Human Warmth & Empathy

No robotic monologues or formal clichés. Calibrated to sound and feel like a real conversational peer.

Privacy & Local Persistence

Your data lives in your local SQLite and offline embeddings. Zero tracking, zero third-party telemetry leakage.

Proactive Momentum

Goals aren't just wishlists—they are systematically broken into phased milestones and verified daily directives.

Dual Neural Core

Powered by local Ollama models (Qwen, Gemma, LLaMA) with seamless ultra-fast cloud Groq failover.

Help Center & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating, conversing with, and maximizing AURA.

Click the microphone icon or tap the Spacebar when not typing in an input field. Speak naturally; when you pause, AURA will automatically process your input, reply with empathetic speech synthesis, and temporarily disable the microphone while speaking to eliminate runaway feedback loops.
In Chrome or Edge, click the tune/padlock icon on the left side of the URL bar (next to http://localhost:8000), select "Site settings", and set Microphone to Allow. Refresh the page to apply the permissions.
When you click "New Goal" and provide your intention, timeline, category, and experience level, AURA's neural engine analyzes your objective and returns a structured roadmap with milestone phases. It simultaneously creates daily directive tasks in your Directives & Tasks list so you always have immediate actions for the day.
Streak Freeze Shields protect your daily momentum if you miss a check-in day. If an active goal misses a day, a shield token is consumed to keep your streak intact. If you run out of shields, AURA offers a quick recovery challenge to restore your streak.
AURA is designed as a local-first platform. All user accounts, chat logs, goals, and uploaded artifacts are stored locally on your machine in the SQLite database and local artifact folders. Document searches are executed locally via vector RAG.
