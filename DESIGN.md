# Design System Specification — AURA
**Theme:** Cyberpunk Deep Glass  
**Color Palette:**
* Singularity Background: `radial-gradient(#150d2a 0%, #070312 100%)`
* Accent Cyan: `#38bdf8` (Primary indicators, active terminals)
* Accent Purple: `#c084fc` (Cognitive memories, LLM routing)
* Accent Amber: `#f59e0b` (Recovery challenges, streak warnings)
* Card Glass: `rgba(18, 12, 38, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
